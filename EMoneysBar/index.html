House rules

A bluffing game of cards and nerve. Lie well, or answer to the revolver.

1

The goal

Be the last player breathing.

2

The setup

Everyone gets 5 cards and a 6-chamber revolver holding one live round. One suit, ♥ ♦ ♣ ♠, is named the table suit for the round.

3

Playing cards

On your turn, play 1-3 cards face down and declare them all to be the table suit. They don't have to be. That's the whole game.

4

Calling "Liar!"

Instead of playing, call out the previous player. The cards are flipped:

  • They lied → they face the revolver.
  • They told the truth → you face the revolver.

Out of cards on your turn? You must call Liar.

If the host opens calls to anyone, every player still in can call out the last play at any time, not just whoever is next. The first call to land counts.

5

Emptying your hand

Play your last card successfully and you still face the revolver, but survive it and the round ends on your terms.

6

The revolver

Spin, pull. A bang eliminates you. A click and you live: the cylinder reloads for the next round, and you start it.
